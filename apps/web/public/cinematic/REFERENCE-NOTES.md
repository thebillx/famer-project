# Cinematic reference record

Observed 23 October 2026 for the isolated `/cinematic-lab` prototype.

## Primary motion reference — Edolus

- URL: https://edolus.com/
- Direct browser observation: staged preloader, full-screen Earth introduction,
  a central initiation control, then a continuous scroll progression from Earth
  to an orbital craft and a bright terrain/data scene.
- Adopted principles: one full-viewport world, sparse telemetry, deliberate holds,
  continuous camera progression and readable centered/offset copy on desktop and
  a 390×844 viewport.
- Rights boundary: visual and cinematography reference only. No Edolus code,
  model, texture, logo, audio, text or claim is included. Source and license were
  not available for reuse.

## Secondary hierarchy reference — ThreeUI Meridian

- URL: https://threeui.com/landing-pages/meridian-page
- Public preview observation: dark Earth/map composition, compact segmented
  navigation, a large left-aligned statement and restrained metric cluster.
- Adopted principle: keep operational labels subordinate to the 3D destination.
- Access boundary: the public page exposes a playable authored preview, not its
  live renderer or source. ThreeUI Pro source, controls and exports were not
  accessed, purchased or copied.
